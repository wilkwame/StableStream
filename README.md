# StableStream – Real-Time USDC Payment Rails

> Stream payroll, remittances, and subscriptions in real-time using Arc Blockchain and USDC — powered by AI agents for automation and optimization.

---

## 🚀 Overview

StableStream is a payment infrastructure that enables **real-time streaming of USDC payments using Arc’s sub-second finality and USDC-native gas.

Employers can automate salaries, freelancers can get paid by the second, and businesses can integrate instant global payments through our API.

---

## 🧠 Why It Matters

- Traditional payroll & remittance systems are slow (3–7 days) and expensive ($25–$50 per transaction).  
- StableStream reduces costs to ~$0.02 and settles payments **instantly** on-chain.  
- Powered by **AI Agents**, payments are automatically scheduled, optimized, and managed intelligently.

---

## ⚙️ Core Features

✅ Real-Time USDC Streaming – Continuous payments with instant settlement  
✅ AI Payroll Agent – Automates scheduling, proration, and optimization  
✅ Arc Integration – Sub-second finality with predictable gas fees  
✅ Circle Programmable Wallets – Seamless custody and UX  
✅ Business API – Simple integration into existing payroll systems  
✅ Mobile Dashboard – Real-time balance updates for employees
